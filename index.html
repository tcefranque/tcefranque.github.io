Tiago Chandiona Ernesto Franque

Ph.D. candidate in Statistics and Probability

T. C. E. Franque is a Ph.D. candidate in Statistics and Probability at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG). His advanced academic research involves proposing structural estimation models designed to optimize decision-making and strategic management systems.

He holds an M.Sc. in Statistics and Probability from the University of Brasília (UnB), where he specialized in core fields such as Statistical Inference, Mathematical Statistics, and Survival Analysis. He completed his Bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Physics from the Universidade Pedagógica de Moçambique.

His primary methodological expertise lies within Regression Analysis, spatio-temporal architectures, and formulation of discrete survival data models with censoring configurations.
