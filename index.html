Ph.D candidate in Statistics and Probability

University of Federal de Minas Gerais|Now

I am currently a candidate for the Ph.D in Statistics and Probability at the Federal University of Minas Gerais. I am currently developing the final thesis entitled: ''M -quantile Regression Estimator to Periodic Spatio-Temporal Poisson -INGARCH model for Count Data''. The Estimator will be applied in real data to model epidemiological situations such as cases of malaria and Dengue as well as other areas that involve count data.
